Beginner Guide

Teens who range from having not run before to going for very occasional, one-off runs (e.g. someone who has only ran during Physical Education, or runs every month.) These are roughly males running a 23 minute 5k and above, and females running a 26 minute 5k and above. If you don’t have a 5k time, chances are you’re a beginner.

Intermediate Guide

Teens who have run structure for a consistent period time (such as a student who runs under a coach’s structured program, or one who aspires to hit a quantifiable target, like distance or time, for x number of weeks.) These are roughly male sub-23-minute 5k runners, or female sub-26-minute 5k runners.
