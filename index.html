About the website

The Runner's Base is a free training resource for teenage cross country and track runners who've moved past "just starting out" but can't find training guidance built for their age, schedule, and event distances. Most intermediate running content online is written for adults training for marathons — The Runner's Base instead explains pace zones, workout types, and race strategy specifically for 5K/XC teen racing, in language that doesn't assume you already know the jargon, backed by interactive tools (pace calculator, training plan generator) and written by someone who's actually raced at this level (2x SEASAC qualifier).
